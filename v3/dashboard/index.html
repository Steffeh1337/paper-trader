Rules in force

The strategy as written in v3/STRATEGY.md, turned into exact rules in v3/ict.py. All times are Romanian time.

Monthly deposit
€— into the euro account
Markets
DAX 40 (GER40 CFD) and Nasdaq-100 (US100 CFD), long and short, one position at a time per index
News filter
No trading on a day with a high-impact (red folder) event: EUR or USD news for the DAX, USD news for the Nasdaq-100. Today: —
Windows
New entries only 10:15–16:15, 16:45–19:00 and 20:00–21:30 (the DAX in all three, the Nasdaq-100 in the New York ones). Pauses: the London open (09:45–10:15), the New York open (16:15–16:45) and the New York lunch (19:00–20:00).
Liquidity
A sweep of the DAX high or low formed until 09:59 (10:15–12:00 only), the previous day's high or low, the day's high or low before the window, or a 5-minute swing high or low from the last two hours
Confirmation
Within an hour of the sweep: a market structure shift (a close beyond the last swing point), a displacement bar (body at least 1.5× the average and 60% of its range) and a fair value gap, in any order
Entry
A limit order at the fair value gap's retest, valid for an hour; cancelled if the price reaches the target or the stop first
Stop and target
Stop beyond the sweep's extreme plus 2 points; target —R

16:15 rule
A position opened before 16:15 stays open only if it is at least +1R; otherwise it is closed at 16:15. Everything still open is closed at 22:55.
