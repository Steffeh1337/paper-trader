Paper Trader

Paper day trading in one account, funded with €1,000 a month. Mornings: the DAX 40 stocks and a DAX ETF, every trading day, from the euro balance. Evenings: the Nasdaq-100 stocks and QQQ, from a dollar balance, only while that session's own last 20 days were profitable. Each session takes up to three breakouts of its opening range in the first two hours, with a stop-loss 1% below the entry and a take-profit 2% above, using fractional shares. Rules in force

Chosen from a backtest of the last 60 trading days and common advice on intraday activity (breakouts in the first two hours, nothing new in the midday lull). The DAX was roughly break-even; the Nasdaq-100 was break-even only from a dollar balance and with a 30-minute range; GBP/USD rarely moves 1–2% a day and is left out.
