Paper Trader

Automatic day trading on paper with the day-trader-skill, funded with €200 a month. Every trading day after the US close, the simulator replays the session on Alpaca's 5-minute bars for the 600 most-traded US stocks priced $5 to $150: it takes the first three 15-minute opening-range breakouts of the day (several at once: best Time Machine score first), targets 2R with the stop at the range midpoint, and fills them from real bars. These rules (variant B) were chosen by the six-month backtest below. The benchmark puts the same money into SPY on the same day.

Backtest: how the rules were chosen

320 rule variants replayed on 132 trading days (April 29 to November 4, 2026), 600 stocks, 27,807 breakouts, the same engine as the live run. The first 79 days picked; the last 53, never used for picking, checked. Returns are on the money deposited (€200 a month, taken as $224), against SPY bought with the same deposits.

VariantFirst 79 daysUnseen 53 daysAll 132 daysSPY, all daysTradesAvg R a tradeWorst drawdown

SPY made +3.6% on the first 79 days and +0.5% on the unseen 53. The variant that earned most on the first 79 days (F) lost on the unseen days, a sign of overfitting. B made money in both periods and roughly tied SPY overall; every variant still averaged slightly below 0R a trade, so none shows a reliable edge.
