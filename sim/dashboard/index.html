Paper Trader

Automatic day trading on paper with the day-trader-skill, funded with €200 a month. Every trading day after the US close, the simulator replays the session: opening-range breakouts at 9:50 New York time, ranked by the Time Machine, filled from real 5-minute bars. The benchmark puts the same money into SPY on the same day.
